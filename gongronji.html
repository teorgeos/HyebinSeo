Slowing Down Still Matters

During the exhibition at the Heeum History Museum of Japanese Military "Comfort Women" in Daegu, it showed that visitors were willing to slow down, read, think, and write. They spent 20 to 30 minutes reading, correcting distorted sentences themselves, and leaving their own record behind. By the end of the exhibition, around 140 new sentences had been added to the archive.
